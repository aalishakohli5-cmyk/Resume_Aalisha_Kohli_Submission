Green Energy GCC Consulting

BreakTheCase ’26 · BITS Hyderabad Consulting Group · 2026

  • Proposed automated submission gates for PO matching, mandatory fields, attachments and duplicate checks.
  • Estimated monthly error costs falling from ₹9.33 lakh to ₹0.36 lakh based on team assumptions, with a control plan and sensitivity analysis.
