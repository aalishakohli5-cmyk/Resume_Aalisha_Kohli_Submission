Green Energy GCC Consulting

BreakTheCase ’26 · BITS Hyderabad Consulting Group · 2026

  • Applied Six Sigma DMAIC to invoice-processing defects across 58 fields and approximately 16.7K invoices. Set a 3.80 sigma baseline from 990 errors in 1,603 audited invoices, and used Pareto and fishbone analysis to trace 94.9% of errors to vendor submission.
  • Proposed automated submission gates for PO matching, mandatory fields, attachments and duplicate checks.
  • Estimated monthly error costs falling from ₹9.33 lakh to ₹0.36 lakh based on team assumptions, with a control plan and sensitivity analysis.

DigiYatra Commercial Expansion

CDPG National Student Case Challenge · IIM Bangalore · 2026

  • Designed a token-based framework for Track 3: Governance & Ecosystem Management, with single-use, purpose-bound tokens and a six-stage partner lifecycle from registration to revocation.
  • Proposed the DigiYatra Foundation as sole regulator and added citizen-control safeguards for consent, visibility, erasure and opting out without losing core travel services.
  • Considered open-standards interoperability in the proposed design.
